# Profile
Massimo Bini is a postdoctoral researcher at the Explainable Machine Learning group. He previously obtained his PhD from the University of Tübingen under the supervision of Prof. Zeynep Akata. Massimo received his Master's degree in Mathematical Engineering and his Bachelor's degree in Engineering Physics, both from the Polytechnic University of Turin.
His main interest lies in leveraging foundation models across different modalities in a scalable and efficient manner.
