# Profile

Ali Kholmovaia is a student researcher at the Explainable Machine Learning Group under the supervision of Prof. Zeynep Akata. She is currently pursuing her Master's degree in Computer Science at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), and holds a Bachelor's in Computer Science from the University of Marburg and a Bachelor's in Philosophy from Saint Petersburg University. Her research focuses on mechanistic interpretability and understanding how information is compressed, stored and represented in neural networks.
