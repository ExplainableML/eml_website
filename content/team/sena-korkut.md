# Profile

Sena Korkut is a Research Scientist at the Explainable Machine Learning (EML) group and Helmholtz Munich, supervised by Prof. Dr. Zeynep Akata. She received her Master’s degree in Informatics from the Technical University of Munich (TUM) in 2026 and her Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Bilkent University, Turkey. Her primary research interests focus on multimodal learning, and reliable evaluation and benchmarking for vision-language models.
