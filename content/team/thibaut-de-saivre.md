# Profile

Thibaut de Saivre is a PhD student under the joint supervision of Florence d'Alché-Buc, Quentin Bouniot and Pavlo Mozharovskyi at Télécom Paris and Zeynep Akata at EML. He holds engineering degrees from École polytechnique and Télécom Paris with a major in AI. He is interested in predictive uncertainty estimation and explainability of neural networks.
