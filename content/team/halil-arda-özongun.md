# Profile
Halil Arda Özongun is an undergraduate researcher at the Explainable Machine Learning Group. He is a senior Computer Science student at Bilkent University. He previously worked with Prof. Aykut Erdem and Prof. Erkut Erdem on diffusion-based generative models. His main interests lie in multimodal large language models, interpretability, and explainable AI.
