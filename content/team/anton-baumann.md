# Profile
Anton Baumann is a master’s thesis student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich and the Technical University of Munich (TUM), under the supervision of Prof. Andreas Krause and Prof. Zeynep Akata. He received his bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from TUM. His primary research interests focus on uncertainty-aware deep learning and improving the efficiency of reinforcement learning for large language models.
