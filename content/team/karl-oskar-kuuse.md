# Profile

Karl Oskar Kuuse is a master's student in Mathematics and Data Science at the Technical University of Munich, currently working on his master's thesis with the Explainable Machine Learning Group led by Prof. Zeynep Akata. He received his bachelor's degree in Mathematics from the University of Tartu, Estonia. His current research focuses on mechanistic interpretability for large language models, dealing more specifically with activation steering and prompt optimization.
