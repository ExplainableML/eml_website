# Profile

Julia Herbst is a master’s student in Informatics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) and a student researcher at the Explainable Machine Learning Group. She received her bachelor’s degree in Informatics from TUM, with an exchange stay at the National University of Singapore. Her primary research interests focus on multimodal learning and understanding model representations.
