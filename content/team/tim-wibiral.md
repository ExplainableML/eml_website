# Profile
Tim Wibiral is a PhD student supervised by Prof. Zeynep Akata. He received his MSc in Artificial Intelligence and BSc in Computer Science from Ulm University. During his studies, he gained research and engineering experience in two academic research groups and at Porsche AG. In addition to his research, Tim is involved in teaching the undergraduate courses PGdP and EidI. His main research interests are test-time training and memorization, Explainability, and AI Safety.
