# Profile

Philipp Spohn is a master's student in Informatics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), currently working on his master's thesis with the Explainable Machine Learning Group under the supervision of Prof. Zeynep Akata. He received his bachelor's degree in Informatics from TUM. His research focuses on adaptive language models, particularly continual learning, personalization, and machine unlearning.
