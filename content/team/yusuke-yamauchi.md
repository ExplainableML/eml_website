# Profile
Yusuke Yamauchi is a visiting researcher in the Explainable Machine Learning group, supervised by Prof. Zeynep Akata. He received his master’s degree from the University of Tokyo, where he is currently pursuing his PhD under the supervision of Prof. Yusuke Miyao. His primary research interest lies in understanding the inner workings of large language models, particularly how complex and diverse concepts are encoded and organized within their representation spaces.
