# CIT423007 - Trustworthy AI for Medicine

## Lecturers
Prof. Zeynep Akata, Dr. Georgios Kaissis, Prof. Daniel Rückert

## Content
This module covers the core concepts of trustworthy AI in medicine:

- Technical robustness and safety: reliable AI design, uncertainty quantification, robustness and verification, safety mechanisms
- Transparency and explainability: XAI methods (e.g., LIME, SHAP), traceability and auditability, transparency of systems
- Ethical, legal, and social implications: fairness and bias mitigation, privacy and data governance, human agency and oversight, accountability and responsibility

<br>

The first part of the lecture is given by the EML Munich group. These lectures introduce the technical foundations of modern large-scale AI (foundation models, adaptation, multimodal alignment, generative models) and connect them to explainability and trustworthiness.

<br>

This is a Master's level course with 4 semester weekly hours, taught in English.
